## 📌 Project Overview
A Data Visualization project analyzing **16,598 video games** sold globally 
from **1980 to 2020** using Microsoft Power BI. This dashboard uncovers 
trends, regional preferences and market leaders across the global 
gaming industry.

## 📈 Key Insights
> 🏆 **Nintendo** leads all publishers with **1.7 billion+** units sold globally

> 🎯 **Action** is the #1 most sold genre worldwide

> 📅 Gaming sales **peaked in 2008-2009** then declined as digital 
distribution took over

> 🌎 **North America** drives nearly **49%** of all global gaming sales

> 🇯🇵 **Japan** uniquely prefers Role-Playing games unlike NA & EU 
which favor Action and Sports

> 🕹️ **PS2** is the highest selling platform of all time
